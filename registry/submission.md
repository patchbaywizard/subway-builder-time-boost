### Description

## Time Boost

Time Boost adds **five extra simulation speed levels beyond Subway Builder's native ultrafast setting**, plus a live **Actual hr/s** readout so you can see how fast your city is really running.

![Time Boost clock and subway icon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/patchbaywizard/subway-builder-time-boost/v1.8.1/time-boost/images/icon.png)

### Controls

- **Boost 1–5:** choose an extra speed level directly from the bottom bar.
- **] / [:** continue beyond native ultrafast through the boost levels, or step back down into the normal game speeds.
- **Reset:** restore the original ultrafast settings.
- **Actual hr/s:** measured in-game hours per real second, averaged over three-second windows. Give it a few seconds after changing levels to settle.

Selecting a boost preserves pause. Loading a city or save clears the boost so you can choose it again when ready. Bracket shortcuts are ignored while typing or using dialogs; custom remapped shortcuts are not extended.

### Performance

The boost levels request more simulation work; they are **not guaranteed speeds**. Large networks and CPU load can limit throughput, and a higher level can sometimes be slower. Use the actual readout to find the best level for your city and computer. The mod runs the game's normal simulation rather than skipping the clock forward.

### Installation and compatibility

Install through Railyard once approved, or download the release ZIP, extract the **time-boost** folder into your game's **mods** directory, restart Subway Builder, and enable **Time Boost** in **Settings → Mods**. Load a city to see the controls.

Requires **Subway Builder 1.7.1 or newer**. Gameplay has been observed on macOS; Windows and Linux remain untested. Other mods that change simulation speed or update batches may conflict. Native View-menu entries are not included.
